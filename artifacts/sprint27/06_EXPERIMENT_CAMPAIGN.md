# Sprint 27 — Experiment Campaign Design (Q6)

**Seven experiments (one free), totaling an estimated 17–26 hours of Apple M4 Metal Performance Shaders compute at single seed — feasible across two to three overnight runs, with explicit prioritization: the two decisive arms run first and the fusion-variant arm can wait for CUDA.** Compute figures extrapolate from the measured anchors in `artifacts/sprint26a/04_COMPUTE_REPORT.md` (V1-scale: 38.2 minutes training + 9–14 minutes evaluation, measured; thermal drift lengthens later runs) with V3-scale runs estimated at 3–5× V1 per the 4,353,217-versus-822,401 parameter ratio and three encoder stacks — the V3-scale numbers are extrapolations, marked as such, not measurements.

**Prerequisite P0 (evidence corpus, no training):** rebuild the aligned audit corpus backward from `data/aditya_l1/processed/` (SoLEXS 915 files, HEL1OS 960 files, Oct/Dec 2023 → Jun 2026) with a flare census and per-event data-quality audit, replacing the 4-day zero-M/X-flare corpus that made all official-target audit evidence vacuous (`artifacts/aditya_l1/target_relationship_audit.json`). Input/output-bound, ~2–4 hours, no model compute. Model experiments run on the existing flare-bearing Stage-2 splits (`artifacts/sprint14c/s2_*.parquet`; test 261,095 windows at 11.92% positive) and do not block on P0, but P0 is mandatory for any *audit-level* value claim.

**Common protocol for all arms:** seed 42 first (additional seeds 43–44 promoted to any arm whose primary metric beats its reference, before any conclusion is drawn — single-seed results are screening only, per the Sprint 26A/26B precedent); evaluation through the frozen Sprint 24 `UnifiedEvaluator` where the V1 pipeline applies, and through the sprint14c evaluation protocol on Stage-2 splits for V3-scale arms; validation-only calibration and threshold selection; Sprint 23 provenance gates on all new artifacts.

**Decision logic:** the campaign question is answered by the ordered comparisons C1-vs-C0 (does physics on GOES alone already capture the headroom?), C5-vs-C4 (does fixing the inputs unlock the instruments?), and C5-vs-GOES-only-on-S2 (net instrument value under fair conditions). If C1 gains exceed C5 gains, Version 4 prioritizes feature engineering over fusion; if C5 clearly exceeds both C4 and GOES-only (multi-seed confirmed), Version 4 prioritizes instrument fusion; if neither moves, the architecture-redesign path from `artifacts/sprint26b/GO_NO_GO_DECISION.md` stands alone.

**Totals and feasibility:** single-seed screening ≈ 17–26 hours M4 (C1 1.5 h; C2–C4 ≈ 7–11 h; C5 ≈ 3.5–5 h; C6 ≈ 3–5 h; P0 2–4 h input/output-bound) — feasible in two to three overnight sessions. **Priority if constrained: C1 and C5 first** (the two arms that decide the V4 direction), C4 second (C5's control), C2/C3 third, **C6 deferred to CUDA access** (new fusion module, highest per-run cost, and meaningful only conditional on C5). Multi-seed confirmation of any winner (3 seeds × winning arms ≈ 8–15 additional hours) follows the Sprint 26 precedent and is where CUDA (`artifacts/sprint26a/04_COMPUTE_REPORT.md` CUDA assessment) would compress days to hours.
