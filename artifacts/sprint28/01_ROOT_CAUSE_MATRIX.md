# Sprint 28 — Root Cause Matrix (Task 1)

**Top-ranked bottleneck: B8, the evidence vacuum — the aligned Aditya-L1 audit corpus contains zero M/X flare events, making every instrument-value claim toward an ISRO operator unsupportable regardless of how good the features, fusion, or models become.** It outranks everything else because operator trust is built on demonstrable evidence, and B8 blocks the existence of that evidence for the mission's flagship instruments; every other bottleneck merely degrades a measurement that B8 prevents from being made at all.

Ranking criterion: expected impact on operator trust (honest evidence → early warning quality → alert usability), not True Skill Score alone. Performance-impact estimates are qualitative and marked `NOT PROVEN` where no measurement exists — the only quantified instrument-level gap in the repository is the single-seed +0.0085 True Skill Score difference (`scientific_validation_report.md` §6).

**Reading the ranking:** the top five are all cheap-to-moderate fixes (B8 data engineering, B9 three features, B5/B2 hygiene, B1 a feature-pipeline build) that jointly enable the fair test; the expensive items (B7, B6) sit at the bottom precisely because Sprint 27 showed they cannot pay off until the top of the table is cleared. This ordering is the skeleton of the Task 6 roadmap.
