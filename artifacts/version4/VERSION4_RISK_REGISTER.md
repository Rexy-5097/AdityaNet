# Version 4 — Risk Register

**Conclusion:** Sixteen registered risks. The three most severe are scientific: the model may genuinely not beat persistence (R1), the extended instrument corpus may still fail to deliver usable joint flare episodes (R2), and the feature set's reliance on flare-history features may make the model a persistence mimic by construction (R3). Four entries (R10, R13–R15) are adversarial-review flaws found and **not fully resolved** in the roadmap, per the brief's requirement to record them. Likelihood/impact are High/Medium/Low judgments; every risk cites its evidence.
