# STD-21 — Reproduction class is declared

Every Evaluation declares `EXACT`, `EQUIVALENT` or `UNREPRODUCIBLE`
([ADR-0021](../adr/ADR-0021.md)). `UNREPRODUCIBLE` evaluations may not be published or
persisted.

`EQUIVALENT` requires two platforms to demonstrate and is recorded as PARTIAL in VVMP §10.2
(U-2); it must be marked unexercised wherever reported.
