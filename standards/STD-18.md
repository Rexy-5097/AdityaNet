# STD-18 — Third-party execution is isolated

Third-party method execution is out-of-process, network-denied and resource-limited
([ADR-0016](../adr/ADR-0016.md)).

> **Status: UNVERIFIABLE.** The isolation infrastructure is not built. Recorded as U-1 in
> VVMP §10.2. This property must not be claimed in published material until it exists and
> is tested.
