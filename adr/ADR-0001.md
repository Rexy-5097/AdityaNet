# ADR-0001 — Product vision and platform strategy

## Context

The project was conceived as a real-time operational flare forecasting platform using
Aditya-L1 data. Investigation established that the public Aditya-L1 archive reaches this
project approximately 33 days late, through a manual ISSDC PRADAN portal session with
per-session credentials, at whole-day granularity. Independently, [L-3](../specs/limitations/L-03.md)
records that no instrument response exists, so calibrated flux and severity are
unconstructible; [L-2](../specs/limitations/L-02.md) records that forecast skill is
horizon-flat, i.e. activity-state persistence rather than precursor detection; and
[L-9](../specs/limitations/L-09.md) records that labels are exogenous GOES.

A prior generation of this project shipped an "Aditya nowcaster" that was, on inspection,
a GOES-flux detector.

NASA CCMC operates an established Flare Scoreboard with registered participants including
national meteorological services.

## Decision

AdityaNet is a **domain-specific platform for solar X-ray flare detection on Aditya-L1
data**. Its mission: develop, evaluate and reproducibly publish flare detection methods on
a canonical, provenance-tracked Aditya-L1 X-ray corpus, such that every claim resolves to
the bytes it came from.

**Non-goals**, binding on every surface:

- Issuing operational space weather forecasts or warnings.
- Competing with NOAA SWPC on forecast accuracy, uptime, or delivery.
- Real-time Aditya-L1 services while the archive remains ~33 days latent.
- Severity or GOES-class estimation from Aditya-L1 (blocked by L-3, permanently, absent an
  instrument response).
- Precursor detection claims (blocked by L-2).
- Publishing forward-looking predictions as a consumable surface on AdityaNet's own
  properties. Predictions are computed, retrospectively published with full provenance,
  and where a forward-looking method exists, submitted to the established community
  scoreboard rather than served independently.
- Alerting, until a consumer is named.
- Generalisation to other scientific domains as a design goal. See [ADR-0025](ADR-0025.md).

## Consequences

- Positioning is complementary to NOAA SWPC, not competitive; the platform consumes SWPC
  data and depends on it for ground truth.
- The scarce assets are domain-specific: the corpus, the archive defect findings, the
  limitation clauses, the negative result.
- Bus factor is 1, and Aditya-L1 acquisition requires a credentialed human. Recorded as an
  accepted risk; no architecture remedies it.
- Success is measured by external model submissions, independent reproductions, dataset
  citations, defects reported back to instrument teams, and the count of published null or
  negative results. Deliberately not throughput or latency.

## Verification

INSP and MAN per VVMP §2.1. Lexicon gate enforces the non-goal vocabulary; a non-goal
audit checklist is executed at each release class (VVMP §9).
