# ADR-0008 — Protocols are immutable pre-registered artifacts

## Context

Pre-registration is meaningless if the protocol is editable prose. A protocol that can be
adjusted after results are seen removes the degree of freedom that pre-registration exists
to remove.

## Decision

A **Protocol** is an immutable, content-addressed artifact carrying: task definition,
splits, metrics, uncertainty estimator, operating points, permitted instruments, label
source, `requires_bitemporal`, and `tolerance`.

A Protocol referenced by an Evaluation must resolve by digest. Registered Protocols cannot
be mutated.

## Consequences

- A result can be checked against the protocol that governed it.
- Changing a protocol means registering a new one, and comparisons across protocol versions
  must say so.

## Verification

INTG and INSP per VVMP §2.1: a mutation-rejection test, and inspection of the commit order
establishing that the protocol predates any fitted method.
