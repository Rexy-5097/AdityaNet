# ADR-0025 — Free seams and paid abstractions

## Context

[ADR-0018](superseded/ADR-0018.md) stated that abstraction requires two instances.
[ADR-0001](ADR-0001.md) requires that a future real-time source be absorbable without
redesign. Read literally, the constitution forbade the decision it required. Two principles
that justify opposite conclusions will be used to justify opposite conclusions.

## Decision

ADR-0018's rule is refined, not repealed:

> **Paid abstractions require two instances. Free seams do not.**

**The test:** a decision is a *free seam* if and only if it would still be the correct design
with exactly one instance, forever. Equivalently — if the hypothetical second instance never
arrives, does the decision cost anything?

| Category | Examples | Rule |
|---|---|---|
| **Free seams** | Naming; dependency direction; field granularity; separating genuinely distinct concepts | Permitted at N=1 |
| **Paid abstractions** | Plugin registries; dispatch layers; generic base classes; config-driven behaviour; indirection for a hypothetical case | **Forbidden until N=2** |

## Consequences

- [ADR-0003](ADR-0003.md) is justified as a free seam, not by future-proofing.
- A reader gets a test to apply rather than two principles to choose between.
