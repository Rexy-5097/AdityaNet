# ADR-0013 — Limitations as versioned clauses

## Context

A caveat restated in three places becomes three caveats, and the softest one wins.

## Decision

Each limitation is a versioned, individually addressable clause with a stable ID
(`L-01` … `L-11`). Model cards, claims and protocols **cite clause IDs**; they never restate
clause text.

There is exactly one copy of every caveat.

## Consequences

- A limitation cannot be softened on its way to a page.
- Citations must resolve, which the link gate enforces.

## Verification

STAT and INSP per VVMP §2.1: a citation-resolution gate, plus a single-copy audit.
