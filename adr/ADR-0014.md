# ADR-0014 — Batch, single-node

## Context

The workload is one archive acquisition per period at whole-day granularity, and — if a
live source is ever added — approximately 1,440 samples per day. There is no concurrency
problem, no throughput problem, and no multi-tenancy problem.

## Decision

The system is a batch pipeline with registries and a static publication surface.

**No microservices. No Kubernetes. No message broker.** Introducing any of them would add
operational surface to solve problems that do not exist, and [ADR-0001](ADR-0001.md)
forbids unowned operational obligations.

## Consequences

- The system cannot absorb high-rate telemetry without a redesign. Accepted knowingly at
  the stated volumes.
- Operational cost approaches zero, which is what makes a bus-factor-1 project survivable.

## Verification

INSP and ARCH per VVMP §2.1: a dependency scan rejects broker and orchestrator imports.
