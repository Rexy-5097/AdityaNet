# ADR-0010 — Method releases are immutable and retrievable

## Context

A benchmark whose methods cannot be retrieved is a report, not a platform. Results cannot
be re-scored, compared across dataset versions, or rolled back.

## Decision

A **MethodRelease** is immutable and content-addressed, carrying the implementation
artifact, parameters, training provenance, and **declared instrument requirements**
([ADR-0011](ADR-0011.md)).

A registered release must re-instantiate from its digest and reproduce identical
predictions on a fixture.

Standing changes are governed by [ADR-0024](ADR-0024.md).

## Consequences

- Every published score can be re-derived from retrievable inputs.
- Serialising and versioning methods is required work that a script-based benchmark avoids.

## Verification

INTG and PROP per VVMP §2.1: a retrieve-and-reproduce property test.
