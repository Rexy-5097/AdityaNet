# ADR-0003 — Source is distinct from Instrument

## Context

The same instrument may be reachable through several channels with different latency,
authority and authentication. SoLEXS today is reachable only through a ~33-day manual
archive. A future real-time feed would be a different channel to the same sensor.

An earlier rationale justified this separation by future-proofing. That rationale
conflicted with the two-instance rule. [ADR-0025](ADR-0025.md) resolves the conflict and
supplies the correct justification, which is recorded below.

## Decision

**Source** and **Instrument** are separate concepts.

- A **Source** is a channel of acquisition: authority, credentials, latency class,
  granularity, retrieval descriptor.
- An **Instrument** is a sensor: physics, calibration state, and the absence of calibration
  where applicable.

Justification is the free-seam test of [ADR-0025](ADR-0025.md): they would be modelled
separately with exactly one source, forever, because conflating a channel with a sensor is
a modelling error independent of how many channels exist.

**This ADR does not authorise** a source-plugin registry, a dispatch layer, or a
configurable adapter framework. Those are paid abstractions and remain forbidden until a
second source exists.

## Consequences

- Registering a future real-time feed is an adapter registration, not a redesign.
- Credentials are confined to the Source adapter and cannot leak into instrument parsing.

## Verification

ARCH and UNIT per VVMP §2.1: a type-separation test and an adapter-registration test.
