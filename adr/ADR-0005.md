# ADR-0005 — Content addressing for every immutable object

## Context

Sequential or timestamp identifiers do not establish that two references denote the same
bytes. A platform whose central claim is byte-level traceability cannot rest on identifiers
that are merely conventional.

## Decision

Every immutable object is identified by the SHA-256 digest of its content. Sequential and
timestamp identifiers are forbidden for immutables.

Mutable *names* — Dataset, Method, Source, Instrument, LabelSource — may carry conventional
identifiers, because they are never referenced by an Evaluation.

Digests are minted by the provenance shared kernel and by nothing else
([ADR-0026](ADR-0026.md)).

## Consequences

- Identity is verifiable by anyone holding the bytes.
- The audit procedure (VVMP §7) is executable without privileged access.

## Verification

PROP and UNIT per VVMP §2.1: a digest-stability property across restarts, orderings and
platforms, plus an identifier-format gate.
