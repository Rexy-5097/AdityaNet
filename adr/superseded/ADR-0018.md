# ADR-0018 — Abstraction requires two instances

> **SUPERSEDED by [ADR-0025](../ADR-0025.md).** Retained unedited. The rule was correct in
> substance but, stated without qualification, contradicted [ADR-0001](../ADR-0001.md).

## Decision (as originally accepted)

No abstraction is introduced to serve a hypothetical second case. Seams are kept clean
because that is free; layers are not added because they are not.

## Why it was superseded

[ADR-0001](../ADR-0001.md) requires that a future real-time source be absorbable without
redesign, while this ADR forbade building for a second instance that did not yet exist.
Read literally, the constitution forbade the decision it required, and the two ADRs could be
used to justify opposite conclusions.

[ADR-0025](../ADR-0025.md) refines rather than repeals: **paid abstractions require two
instances; free seams do not**, with an explicit test for distinguishing them.
