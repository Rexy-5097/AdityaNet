# ADR-0004 — All observations are bitemporal

## Context

Without recording when the system *learned* a fact, "what did we know at time T?" is
unanswerable. Forecast scoring cannot then be leak-free, and any revision of an external
label catalog silently invalidates every historical score. The information cannot be
recovered after the fact, so this decision cannot be retrofitted.

## Decision

Every Observation and every Label records **two** times:

- `valid_time` — when the phenomenon occurred.
- `ingest_time` — when this system learned it.

`ingest_time` is stamped at the acquisition boundary. This is the single sanctioned clock
read in the system; determinism elsewhere is governed by [ADR-0021](ADR-0021.md).

Nullability and the treatment of pre-bitemporal data are governed by
[ADR-0022](ADR-0022.md).

## Consequences

- The leakage gate becomes implementable.
- A label revision produces a new release without invalidating prior scores.
- Migration is the highest-risk phase of implementation; the procedure is
  [ADR-0027](ADR-0027.md).

## Verification

PROP and INTG per VVMP §2.1: a "both times present" property test and a dual-write
integration test.
